How to play

Steer: hold the left or right side of the screen. On a keyboard, use the arrow keys or A and D.

Nitro: hold the red button (or Space). It uses the boost bar.

Fill the bar: grab coins and squeeze past cars. Close calls build your score multiplier.

Crash: hitting a car or the barrier ends the run. You keep every coin you picked up.
